Adobe MAX 2025

The creativity conference.

Adobe MAX is Adobe’s annual celebration of creativity — a high-energy conference where imagination meets innovation. It’s where Adobe unveils the lxatest tools and features in their creative suite, and artists, designers, and dreamers from around the world come together to get inspired, connect, and create. This year I was lucky enough to attend and below are the key take-aways from the sessions I attended.

Opening Keynote

Explore the latest product announcements, features, and innovations that will help you bring your creative vision to life. Adobe leaders, including Shantanu Narayen, chair and chief executive officer, and David Wadhwani, president, digital media business, preview what’s ahead.

Create Editable Textures and Graphic Styles the DKNG way

Nathan & Dan - DKNG Studio CD and Illustrator

  • Keep things live. From live text to live textures keep things as live as you can in case of things need to change.
  • Global Colors - to speed up effeciency in Illustrator use global colors so when you change one the rest of your canvas changes...easier than select fill all.
  • Guidelines - anything can be turned into a guide in addition to the ones you can pull out of rulers. Create shape and CMD + 5 to turn it into a guide. Can move anywhere on canvas.
  • DKNG's Deck and Textures

Turbocharge Your Illustrator Game: Speed, Shortcuts, & Optimization

James Bernard, Logo & Visual Identity Designer, Barnard Co

  • Effeciency - figure out how you can create shortcuts/action to speed up your workflow for the things you do on a daily basis. Creating a shortcut for something you go into the menus for can save seconds everyday but days over a career.
  • Hardware - Prefers customizable hardware - Logitech and Marcopad!
  • AI File - in addition to the deck the AI file is in the CTA where he shows us everything we went over inside the layer comps.

After Effects for Designers: From Static to Motion

Luisa Winters, Motion Design & Video Editing Educator, Adobe Community Expert

  • Prep your artwork - AE works in layers just like PS and AI so when importing your artwork into AE preserve the layers so each one can animate. Keep in mind when desiging in AI and PS what you would like animated.
  • Keyframes and motion paths - every section of the animation needs a keyframe.
  • Take your time and learn!
